## Content Strategy Framework

### Content Funnel
| Stage | Goal | Content Types | Metrics |
|-------|------|---------------|---------|
| **Awareness** | Attract new audience | Blog posts, social content, videos, podcasts | Traffic, impressions, reach |
| **Consideration** | Educate and build trust | Guides, case studies, webinars, comparison pages | Time on page, email signups, downloads |
| **Decision** | Convert to customer | Product demos, free trials, testimonials, pricing pages | Conversion rate, qualified leads |
| **Retention** | Keep and grow customers | Newsletters, tutorials, community content | Engagement, NPS, expansion revenue |

### Content Brief Template
1. **Topic**: What is this content about?
2. **Target Audience**: Who are we writing for? (persona, job title, pain points)
3. **Search Intent**: Informational / Commercial / Navigational / Transactional
4. **Primary Keyword**: Main SEO target (search volume, difficulty)
5. **Secondary Keywords**: Related terms to include naturally
6. **Content Format**: Blog post / Landing page / Email / Social post
7. **Word Count Target**: Based on SERP analysis
8. **CTA**: What action should the reader take?
9. **Internal Links**: Link to related existing content

### SEO Checklist
- [ ] Primary keyword in title (H1)
- [ ] Primary keyword in first 100 words
- [ ] Meta description under 160 characters with keyword
- [ ] Headers (H2, H3) use related keywords naturally
- [ ] Images have descriptive alt text
- [ ] Internal links to 2-3 related pages
- [ ] External links to authoritative sources
- [ ] URL slug is short and contains keyword

### Email Marketing Rules
- Subject lines: 6-10 words, create curiosity or urgency
- Preview text: Complement the subject line, don't repeat
- One clear CTA per email
- Mobile-first design (60%+ of opens are mobile)
- Personalization beyond just first name — use behavior data
