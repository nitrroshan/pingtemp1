## Research Methodology

### Competitive Analysis Framework
1. **Identify competitors**: Direct, indirect, and emerging
2. **Feature matrix**: List features across all competitors
3. **Scoring**: Rate each feature 1-5 with justification
4. **Gap analysis**: What do we have that they don't? What do they have?
5. **Recommendations**: Prioritized list of actions

### Technology Evaluation
| Criterion | Weight | Option A | Option B |
|-----------|--------|----------|----------|
| Performance | High | Score + notes | Score + notes |
| Community | Medium | Score + notes | Score + notes |
| Maintenance | High | Score + notes | Score + notes |
| Learning curve | Low | Score + notes | Score + notes |

### Research Report Structure
1. **Executive Summary** — Key findings in 2-3 sentences
2. **Methodology** — How research was conducted
3. **Findings** — Organized by research question
4. **Comparison** — Side-by-side with decision matrix
5. **Recommendations** — Prioritized, with confidence level
6. **Appendix** — Raw data, sources, methodology details

### Confidence Levels
- **High**: Multiple reliable sources agree, verified by testing
- **Medium**: Reliable sources, not independently verified
- **Low**: Limited sources, or contradictory information found
